# Hardware Chatroom · 硬件唠嗑室

**Talk about hardware. Build something fun.**  
**聊聊硬件，动手做点好玩的。**

Hardware Chatroom is a curiosity-driven space for people who like to take things apart, figure out how they work, and build something fun. We document the process and thinking behind solving hardware problems, out in the open—from the first “what if?” to the next “it works!”

硬件唠嗑室是一个由好奇心驱动的空间，留给喜欢拆拆东西、琢磨原理、动手做点好玩东西的人。我们公开记录解决硬件问题的过程与思考，从一句“要不试试？”到下一次“跑通了！”

## What we're into · 我们在折腾什么

- **Hardware Hacking** — Take things apart, explore how they work, and see what else they can do.  
  **硬件探索与改造** — 拆开看看，弄懂原理，再试试它还能做什么。
- **AI × Hardware** — Give AI sensors, buttons, and a place in the physical world. Build it, try it, and see what holds up.  
  **AI × 硬件** — 给 AI 接上传感器、按钮和真实世界，做出来、试一试，看看哪些想法经得起验证。
- **DIY & Prototyping** — Turn a “what if?” into a prototype you can put on the workbench.  
  **DIY 与原型制作** — 把脑洞变成能摆上工作台的原型。
- **Open Hardware** — Share designs, code, build notes, and lessons so others can reproduce and build on them.  
  **开放硬件** — 分享设计、代码、制作笔记与经验，让别人也能复现、接着改进。

## How we roll · 我们怎么玩

Curiosity comes first. When something catches our eye—even the latest hype—we'd rather reproduce it, test it, and find out what's actually fun or useful than just pass the news along.

好奇心优先。遇到感兴趣的东西，哪怕是最近的热点，我们也更想亲手复现、测试，弄清楚它到底哪里好玩、哪里有用，而不只是转发新闻。

The messy bits belong here too: the things that worked, the failed attempts, the debugging detours, and the questions still open. A finished build is great; an honest trail of how we got there is worth sharing too.

这里也留给折腾中的各种状况：成功的尝试、失败的实验、排查问题时绕过的弯路，以及还没想明白的问题。成品值得展示，走到成品的真实过程也值得分享。

Bring a question, a weird idea, or a half-working prototype. Let's talk, tinker, and share what we learn.

带着问题、奇怪的点子，或者只跑通一半的原型来吧。一起唠嗑、动手，把学到的东西分享出来。
